Once a month

One letter, never a feed

A new sequence, one pose broken down properly, and a short note about practicing in whatever season we are in. That is the whole email.

One email, first week of the month No lists sold or shared Unsubscribe in one click

What is actually in it

  • One new sequence, written out pose by pose with hold times, before it goes on the site.
  • One pose broken down, with entry steps, alignment cues, modifications and contraindication notes.
  • A short seasonal note, usually two or three paragraphs about practicing in the conditions of the month. Cold floors in January. Practicing while traveling in July.
  • Occasionally, a note about a prop we have found useful, marked clearly if any link in it is an affiliate link.

What is not in it

  • No daily emails. One a month, sent in the first week.
  • No health, medical or therapeutic claims. This is a movement education newsletter.
  • No weight-loss content, no diet content, no body-transformation content, ever.
  • No selling your address. We do not sell or share subscriber lists with anyone.
  • No countdown timers, no fake scarcity, no urgency framing.

How we handle your email address

We collect your email address, and optionally your first name and practice stage, for the sole purpose of sending you the monthly newsletter. We store it with our email service provider. We do not sell it, rent it or share it with advertisers.

In line with the CAN-SPAM Act, every email we send identifies Aruna Yoga Method LLC as the sender, carries our physical mailing address, and includes a working unsubscribe link that we honor within ten business days. You can also unsubscribe at any time by emailing hello@arunayoga.com with the word unsubscribe in the subject line.

Full details of what we collect and why are in our Privacy Policy. This is a double opt-in list, so you will receive one confirmation email and nothing further until you click the link in it.

Rather not give us your email

Entirely reasonable. Everything the newsletter contains ends up on the site within a month, free to read and with no account required. The newsletter is a convenience, not a paywall.
