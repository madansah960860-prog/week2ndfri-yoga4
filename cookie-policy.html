Cookie Policy

What we store on your device, why, and how to change it at any time.

Effective date: 09/01/2026. Last updated: 09/01/2026.

1. The short version

  • We set no non-essential cookies before you choose. On your first visit a banner appears with three options: Accept all, Reject non-essential, and Manage preferences. Nothing beyond strictly necessary storage happens until you pick one.
  • Essential storage remembers your choice. That is all it does.
  • Analytics and advertising cookies are off unless you turn them on.
  • You can change your mind at any time by selecting here or in the footer of any page.

2. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage and tracking pixels. This policy covers all of them, and uses the word cookie for simplicity.

3. Categories we use

3.1 Strictly necessary. Always on.

These are required for the site to work and cannot be switched off, because without them we cannot remember that you asked us not to set anything else.

4. Third-party advertising, including Google

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of many vendors at once at optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Google explains how it uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not use cookies to build a profile of your health, and we never infer health information about you.
  • We do not use fingerprinting techniques to identify your device.
  • We do not set advertising cookies on our legal pages or our 404 page.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money. See the Do Not Sell or Share section of our Privacy Policy.

6. Changing your choice

  • On this site. Select here or in the footer of any page. The banner reopens and your new choice replaces the old one.
  • In your browser. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. Look under Settings, then Privacy. Blocking strictly necessary storage may stop parts of this site from working, including our ability to remember that you declined.
  • Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends a GPC signal we treat it as an opt-out of sale and sharing under California law.

7. Changes to this policy

If we add or remove a cookie we will update the tables above and change the Last updated date. Where a change requires fresh consent, the banner will reappear.
