Partner with Aruna

Our rules, not a deck

Advertising, sponsorship, directory placement and writing. Our rules are on this page rather than in a deck, so you can decide before you write to us.

Five ways to work with us No health claims, ever, from anyone Five business day reply

Who reads this site

Audience figures, traffic data and demographic breakdowns will be published here once we have a full quarter of verified analytics. We will not quote numbers we cannot substantiate, and we will not estimate.

What we can describe accurately is the editorial context. This is a written reference site for people practicing yoga at home, largely in the evening, largely alone, and largely in their first two years of practice. Readers arrive looking for instructions on a specific pose or a sequence of a specific length. Sessions tend to be long and pages tend to be read end to end rather than skimmed.

What we will not accept, from anyone

These apply to display advertising, sponsored content, affiliate partnerships and directory listings without exception.

  • No health, medical or therapeutic claims. No product, article or listing may state or imply that a pose, sequence, breathing technique, supplement or device treats, cures, heals, relieves or prevents any condition.
  • No weight-loss, body-transformation or slimming content. No calorie figures, no fat-burning language, no before-and-after imagery, no body-shape commentary.
  • No diet, cleanse, detox, fasting or supplement products.
  • No fake urgency, countdown timers, fake scarcity, invented statistics or unsubstantiated superlatives.
  • No interstitials, pop-ups, pop-unders, autoplay audio or forced redirects.
  • No creative that mimics editorial content or the site interface.
  • No claims of accreditation, certification or lineage that cannot be verified.

We would rather turn down a placement than run one that puts a reader at risk or puts our advertising review at risk.

Rates and how to pitch

Display advertising

Where ads appear

  • One 728 by 90 slot in the middle of the content on editorial pages, below the main content of the fold.
  • Slots are labeled Advertisement, are visually distinct from editorial content, and never sit adjacent to navigation or buttons.
  • Space is reserved in the layout so nothing shifts as ads load.
  • No ads on legal pages or the 404 page. No ads above the main content anywhere.

How to buy

  • Programmatic inventory runs through Google and our other ad partners.
  • Direct placements are available. Email partners@arunayoga.com for a current rate card.

Sponsored content

How it works

  • A sponsored article carries a clear Sponsored label at the very top of the page, above the headline, in the same clay-coloured frame used for our other labels. The label is permanent and is never removed later.
  • The article follows every editorial rule on this page, including the ban on health claims.
  • We write it. You brief us, you review it for factual accuracy about your product, and you can decline to run it. You cannot rewrite our copy.
  • Sponsored articles are marked with rel attributes on outbound links in line with search engine guidance.
  • We disclose the commercial relationship in the first paragraph as well as in the label.

What we will not write

  • Anything implying a product treats a condition.
  • Comparative claims we cannot substantiate.
  • A review presented as independent when it is paid. If it is paid, it says so at the top.

Affiliate partnerships

How we work

  • We join affiliate programs for props we would set up in our own practice space: mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets.
  • Every affiliate link is disclosed beside the link itself, at the point of use, not only in the footer.
  • We list a free household substitute alongside every prop we mention.
  • Commission rates never influence which product we recommend, and we do not accept payment in exchange for a recommendation.

What this means for you

  • We will not write a positive review to order. If we do not think a product is worth a reader's money, we do not link it.
  • Read our full Advertising & Affiliate Disclosure before you write to us.

Writing for us

What we commission

  • Pose breakdowns in our house format: entry steps, alignment cues, breath pattern, hold time, common mistakes, at least two accessible modifications, one progression and contraindication notes.
  • Sequences with total duration, difficulty, props, a pose-by-pose order with hold times and a beginner path.
  • Long-form guides on habit, home setup, props and practicing in ordinary conditions.

What we ask of writers

  • A current teaching qualification, which we will name on the piece with the awarding body and year.
  • Willingness to write inside our editorial rules, particularly the ban on health claims and weight-loss framing.
  • Chair, wall and prop options written as equal alternatives in every piece.

Rates and how to pitch

  • Pose guides: $150 to $250 per piece in USD, depending on length and research.
  • Sequences: $200 per sequence in USD.
  • Long-form guides of 1,200 words or more: $300 to $450 in USD.
  • Pitch in three or four sentences to editorial@arunayoga.com. Tell us the piece, why you are the person to write it, and what your qualification is. We reply to every pitch within five business days.

Partnership enquiry

Include your product or service, your timeline and your budget range if you have one.

What happens after you send it

  1. We read your enquiry

    Every enquiry reaches our small partnerships team the same day.

  2. We check it against our rules

    Health claims, weight-loss framing and diet content are turned down before anything else happens.

  3. We reply with specifics

    Within five business days, with rates and next steps for the lane you chose.
