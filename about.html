About us

Who we are, and how we work.

A small publisher of written yoga reference material for people practicing at home — before work, after work, or whenever the day allows it.

What we are

Aruna Yoga Method is a written library. We publish pose breakdowns, timed sequences, breath practice instructions and reference material for people who practice at home. We are a publisher, not a studio, and we are not a clinical or medical service of any kind.

The site is deliberately narrow. We do not do video, we do not run classes, and we do not sell a lifestyle. We write instructions carefully, and we write them so that a person on the floor of a small room with two pillows and a chair can follow them.

Why the site looks like this

Most of our readers open this site in very different light: a kitchen floor at 7am with the blinds half up, a lunch break at a desk, a bedroom floor at 11pm once the day is finally done. We did not want to design for just one of those, so we did not. The page reads warm and calm in daylight and switches automatically to a low-glare dark version when your system is set to dark mode — no toggle to find, nothing to configure.

The type is set for reading rather than scanning, and the hold timer on every page dims the screen a little while a hold is running, then brings it back the moment the hold ends. That is a focus feature, not a look. It exists to solve the actual problem of a long hold: you stop watching the clock. It behaves the same way whether your screen started out light or dark.

Who writes it

Aruna Yoga Method is written and edited by a small editorial team based in Asheville, North Carolina. Every pose and sequence page is reviewed before publication by a qualified yoga teacher.

Teacher and reviewer credentials shown on this site are placeholders pending real, verifiable qualifications. We will publish named credentials with awarding body and year once they are in place, and we will not describe anyone on this site as a medical, physical therapy or clinical authority, because nobody here is one.

We are not an accrediting body. We do not verify the qualifications of teachers in our directory, and we say so on that page.

Our editorial rules

These are the rules we hold ourselves to, and we would rather publish them than ask you to take them on trust.

  • No health, medical or therapeutic claims. We describe what a pose does physically, which muscles lengthen and which joints move. We never say a pose treats, cures, relieves or prevents anything.
  • No weight-loss framing. No calorie figures, no fat-burning language, no before-and-after content, no body-shape commentary, no pairing practice with dieting, fasting, cleanses or detox content of any kind.
  • Modifications are equals. Chair, wall and prop-supported options appear in every pose guide as alternatives, not as consolation versions.
  • Contraindications on every pose. Every pose page lists who should approach with care and tells you to work with a qualified teacher for hands-on guidance.
  • No invented credentials, reviews or testimonials. If we have not verified something, we do not publish it as fact.
  • Disclosure at the point of use. Affiliate links are disclosed beside the link. Sponsored articles are labeled at the top of the page.

How the business works

We are open about this because you should know what a page is doing before you read it.

  • Display advertising. The free library is paid for by clearly labeled ads. They never sit above the main content, never touch navigation or buttons, and never appear on legal pages or the 404 page.
  • Optional membership, $6 per month or $54 per year in USD, which removes ads and adds downloadable PDFs, printable pose cards and saved collections. Free content stays free.
  • Affiliate commissions on props we link to, disclosed at every link.
  • Paid digital products, such as downloadable beginner programs and sequence bundles.
  • Sponsored content, labeled at the very top of the page, following the same editorial rules as everything else.
  • Teacher directory listings, free to list, with an optional paid featured placement that is always labeled.

What we will not do

  • We will not run pop-ups, interstitials, pop-unders, automatic downloads or forced redirects.
  • We will not disable your back button or block right-click.
  • We will not autoplay audio.
  • We will not publish fake urgency, fake scarcity, countdown timers or invented statistics.
  • We will not sell or rent your email address.
  • We will not accept a sponsorship that requires a health claim.

Corrections

If something on this site is wrong, tell us. Cueing errors, Sanskrit transliteration mistakes, a contraindication we have missed, a broken link. Email editorial@arunayoga.com or use the contact form and choose Report a correction.

We fix errors on the page itself and note the date the page was last updated in its structured data. We do not quietly rewrite a page that gave incorrect safety guidance without saying so.
