Advertising and affiliate disclosure

How this site makes money, said plainly and in one place, in line with the Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides.

Effective date: 09/01/2026. Last updated: 09/01/2026.

The one-sentence version

Aruna Yoga Method is free to read and is paid for by display advertising, an optional membership, affiliate commissions on props we link to, paid digital products, clearly labeled sponsored articles and paid featured placements in our teacher directory.

1. Display advertising

  • We run display advertising from Google and other advertising partners on editorial pages.
  • Ad slots are labeled Advertisement, are visually distinct from editorial content, and sit within a bordered container so you always know where content ends and advertising begins.
  • Ads never appear above the main content of a page, never sit adjacent to navigation or buttons, and are never styled to resemble editorial content or the site interface.
  • We keep ad density well below content density. There is one in-content slot per page.
  • No ads appear on our legal pages or on our 404 page.
  • Space is reserved in the layout so nothing moves as ads load.
  • We do not control which specific ads are shown by programmatic partners, and an ad appearing here is not an endorsement by us. If you see an ad that breaks our rules, tell us at partners@arunayoga.com and we will block it.
  • Advertising cookies are only set with your consent. See our Cookie Policy.

2. Affiliate links

Some links to props such as mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets are affiliate links. If you buy through one of them, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

How we disclose them

  • At the point of use. Every page that contains affiliate links carries a disclosure beside the links themselves, not only in the footer. You should never have to scroll to the bottom of a page to find out that a link is commercial.
  • The disclosure appears before or immediately next to the link, in plain language, in text you can actually read.
  • Where required, affiliate links carry appropriate rel attributes.

Our rules for ourselves

  • We only link to props we would set up in our own practice space.
  • We list a free household substitute for every prop we mention. Our props guide gives an alternative you already own for every item on the list.
  • Commission rates never influence which product we recommend or how we rank it.
  • We do not accept payment in exchange for a positive review, and we do not publish reviews we have not formed independently.
  • If we would not spend our own money on something, we do not link it.

The specific affiliate programs we participate in will be named here before launch. Any program requiring particular disclosure wording will have that wording added exactly as required.

3. Sponsored content

  • Occasionally a brand pays us to publish an article. When that happens the page carries a clear Sponsored label at the very top, above the headline, before any content.
  • That label is permanent. We do not remove it later, and we do not quietly convert a sponsored page into an editorial one.
  • The commercial relationship is also stated in the first paragraph of the article itself.
  • Sponsored articles follow every editorial rule that applies to the rest of the site, including our absolute ban on health, medical and therapeutic claims and on weight-loss framing.
  • We write the copy. The sponsor briefs us, reviews it for factual accuracy about their product, and may decline to run it. They cannot rewrite our words.
  • Outbound links in sponsored articles carry appropriate rel attributes.

4. Membership and digital products

  • Membership costs $6 per month or $54 per year in United States dollars. It removes advertising and adds downloadable sequence PDFs, printable pose cards and saved collections.
  • We also sell one-time downloadable programs and sequence bundles. Prices are listed on the pricing page.
  • Nothing that is free today moves behind the membership later. The pose library, all sequences, the glossary, the props guide and every article stay free.

5. Teacher directory placements

  • A standard listing in our teacher directory is free. Payment is never a condition of being listed.
  • A featured placement costs $25 per month in United States dollars and appears above standard listings in its city.
  • Featured listings carry a visible Featured label so you can always tell which listings are paid.
  • We do not verify teacher credentials and we do not endorse any listed teacher. We say so on the directory page.
  • We do not publish reviews or star ratings, because we cannot authenticate them.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • We do not publish invented testimonials, reviews, ratings or credentials.
  • We do not run pop-ups, pop-unders, interstitials, automatic downloads, autoplay audio or forced redirects.
  • We do not use countdown timers, fake scarcity or invented statistics.
  • We do not accept advertising or sponsorship for diet, cleanse, detox, fasting, weight-loss or supplement products.
  • We do not accept any placement that would require us to make a health, medical or therapeutic claim.

7. Your trust is the product

The reason this page exists in this much detail is straightforward. A yoga reference site is only useful if you believe the instructions were written to help you practice rather than to move you toward a purchase. Every commercial arrangement above is disclosed where you encounter it, not buried here.

If you ever find a page where a commercial relationship is not obvious, that is a mistake on our part and we want to know about it.

8. Contact

Questions about a commercial arrangement

Questions about advertising, sponsorship or affiliate arrangements go to partners@arunayoga.com, or Aruna Yoga Method LLC, 88 Calder Street, Studio 4, Portland, OR 97209, United States, +1 (503) 555-0142. See also Work With Us.
