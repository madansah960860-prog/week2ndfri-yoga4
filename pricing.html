Free vs membership

The short version

Everything on this site that teaches you something is free to read, with no account and no paywall.

That includes all thirty-four poses, all four sequences, the breathwork page, the glossary, the props guide and every article.

The free version is paid for by display advertising. If you would rather not see ads, or you want printable and downloadable material, membership costs $6 per month or $54 per year in US dollars.

Nothing that is free today will move behind the membership later. If we ever change that, existing readers keep what they had.

In full detail

How this site is funded

There are six revenue streams and here is exactly how each one works.

We would rather say this openly than have you work it out.

  1. Display advertising

    We run display ads from Google and other ad partners on non-legal pages. Ad slots are labeled Advertisement, are visually distinct from editorial content, never appear above the main content of a page, never sit adjacent to buttons or navigation, and never appear on our legal pages or our 404 page. Ad density is kept well below content density. Membership removes them.

  2. Membership

    $6 per month or $54 per year in USD. Members get an ad-free site and downloadable material. Membership never gates educational content that was previously free.

  3. Affiliate commissions

    When we link to props such as mats, blocks, straps and bolsters, some of those links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you buy through them at no additional cost to you. We disclose this next to each link at the point of use, not only in the footer. We list a free household substitute for every prop we mention. We do not accept payment in exchange for a recommendation.

  4. Paid digital products

    One-time purchases, listed above. These are additional material, not a paywall around existing pages.

  5. Sponsored content

    Occasionally a brand pays for an article. When that happens, the page carries a clear sponsored label at the very top, above the headline, and the label stays there permanently. Sponsored articles follow exactly the same editorial rules as everything else on this site, including no health claims. We turn down sponsorships that would require us to break those rules.

  6. Teacher directory listings

    Teachers can list themselves in our directory free of charge. There is a paid featured placement tier that positions a listing higher on the page. Featured listings are labeled as featured so you always know which is which. Payment never affects whether a teacher can appear.

That is the complete list. If a page ever earns us money in a way that is not covered above, it will say so on the page.
