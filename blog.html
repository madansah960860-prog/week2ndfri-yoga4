Why we write

The other half of a home practice

Our pose and sequence pages are reference material. They tell you what to do. These guides cover what determines whether you actually do it.

We write about habit design, about the specific moments a home practice tends to fall apart, about props and how to use them well, and about practicing in ordinary conditions: small rooms, shared spaces, tired evenings and interrupted weeks.

What we do not write about is what yoga will do for your health. That is a medical question, and this site is a movement education site. Every article here describes what a shape or a habit involves, not what it supposedly fixes.

New guides are published roughly once a month and go out first to the newsletter.
