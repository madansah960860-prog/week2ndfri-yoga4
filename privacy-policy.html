Privacy Policy

How Aruna Yoga Method LLC collects, uses, shares and retains personal information, and the choices you have.

Effective date: 09/01/2026. Last updated: 09/01/2026.

1. Who we are

This site is operated by Aruna Yoga Method LLC, 88 Calder Street, Studio 4, Portland, OR 97209, United States. You can reach us at privacy@arunayoga.com or +1 (503) 555-0142. Aruna Yoga Method LLC is the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

This policy applies to www.arunayoga.com and to any email we send you. It does not apply to third-party websites we link to, which have their own policies.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, optional phone number, the topic you select and the content of your message.
  • Newsletter signup. Your email address, and optionally your first name and the practice stage you select.
  • Teacher directory listing request. Your name, business name, email, phone, city and state, and the training details you supply.
  • Partnership enquiry. Your name, company, work email and the details of your enquiry.
  • Membership, when available. Your email address and account credentials. Payment card details are handled by our payment processor and are never stored on our servers.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page, pages requested, and the date and time of the request.
  • Analytics data, only if you consent to analytics cookies. Pages viewed, time on page, approximate location at city level, device type and referral source.
  • Advertising data, only if you consent to advertising cookies. Described in section 4.

2.3 Information stored on your device but not sent to us

  • Your cookie consent choice is stored in your browser's local storage under the key aruna_consent_v1. It stays on your device.
  • Hold timer and sequence player state exists only in the page while you are on it and is discarded when you leave.

4. Cookies, tracking and advertising

Full detail on every cookie category is in our Cookie Policy. In summary:

  • We set no non-essential cookies before you choose. On your first visit a banner offers Accept all, Reject non-essential, and Manage preferences. Nothing beyond strictly necessary storage is set until you make a choice.
  • Essential storage remembers your consent choice and keeps forms secure. It cannot be switched off because the site cannot function without it.
  • Analytics cookies tell us which pages are read. Off by default.
  • Advertising cookies let advertising partners deliver and measure ads. Off by default.

4.1 Google and advertising cookies

We use Google, and may use other third-party advertising vendors, to serve ads on this site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at optout.aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • You can review how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can also reject advertising cookies on this site at any time by selecting Cookie preferences in the footer of any page.

We honor Global Privacy Control signals where our platform supports them, and we treat a GPC signal as a request to opt out of sale or sharing under California law.

6. How long we keep it

  • Contact form messages: 24 months from the date of your last message, then deleted.
  • Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe, plus 12 months in a suppression list so we do not email you again by mistake.
  • Directory listing data: for as long as the listing is live, plus 12 months.
  • Partnership enquiries: 24 months.
  • Server logs: 90 days.
  • Analytics data: 14 months.
  • Membership and transaction records: 7 years, to meet United States tax and accounting requirements.

7. How we protect it

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to personal information is limited to people who need it to do their work. Payment details are handled by our payment processor and never touch our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify you and the relevant authorities of a breach affecting your information where the law requires it.

8. Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults. In line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age. We do not direct this site to children, and we do not knowingly permit anyone under 13 to subscribe to our newsletter, submit a form or create a membership.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact privacy@arunayoga.com and we will delete it promptly. Parents and guardians may request deletion of a child's information at the same address.

9. Your California privacy rights, CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights.

  • Right to know. What personal information we have collected about you, the categories of source, the business purpose, and the categories of third party we disclose it to.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, including sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as that term is defined by the CPRA.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not deny you service, charge you a different price or provide a lower quality of service because you exercised a privacy right.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. However, the use of third-party advertising cookies may be treated as sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. To opt out:

  • Select here or in the footer of any page, then choose Reject non-essential, or turn Advertising off and save.
  • Or enable Global Privacy Control in your browser. We treat that signal as an opt-out request.
  • Or email privacy@arunayoga.com with the subject line Do Not Sell or Share.

We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and complete verified requests within 45 days, extendable once by a further 45 days where permitted. You may use an authorized agent, and we may ask for proof of that authorization.

10. Other United States state privacy rights

Residents of states including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and Montana have comparable rights to access, correct, delete and obtain a portable copy of their personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Use the same contact route above and tell us which state you reside in. If we decline a request you may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will respond to an appeal within 45 days.

11. Note for visitors from the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

This site is aimed at readers in the United States, but it is reachable from anywhere. If you are in the EEA or the UK, the General Data Protection Regulation gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@arunayoga.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

Personal information you provide is processed in the United States. Where we transfer data out of the EEA or the UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful transfer mechanism with the relevant service provider.

12. Third-party links

This site links to other websites, including affiliate links to retailers. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Read the privacy policy of any site you visit from here.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the Last updated date at the top of the page. If the change is significant we will say so on the site, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent again.

14. How to contact us about your data

Questions about your data

Email privacy@arunayoga.com, call +1 (503) 555-0142, or write to Aruna Yoga Method LLC, 88 Calder Street, Studio 4, Portland, OR 97209, United States. You can also use our contact form and select Privacy or data request.
